0.1.1 (2026-11-06)
- Making a game: pressing a Make button with an empty description now tells you to describe the game first (before, nothing seemed

0.1.0 (2026-11-06)
- First version: your own public web arcade. Describe a game on your local page and your assistant or IDE makes it; test it, and
  publish it if you like it. Visitors sign up, play, and see play counts and leaderboards. Two demo games are included, not
  public until you publish them. Needs gateway 1.1.27 or newer.

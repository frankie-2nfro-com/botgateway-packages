SmartHomeControl - change log
The top section is the coming version (no date): write each change below it as "- ...", in words
for the gateway owners - the Portal and the gateway show them as "What's new" before an update.
store_update.sh --package smarthomecontrol publishes it (dated) and puts the next patch version on top; change
the top version by hand for a bigger step (e.g. 1.0.0). This file is not packed into the package.

- [Changed and non commited item put here]

0.1.3 (2026-11-03)
- home_snapshot says when the picture is from (picture_updated_at) and, for cameras that only give their last recording (e.g. battery doorbells like Ring), that it is not live - so the assistant doesn't call an old picture "now"
- The doorbell pop-up also knows Ring's "ding" event

0.1.2 (2026-11-03)
- New tool home_snapshot: the assistant can see a camera's picture now (only cameras it may read), e.g. to answer "is someone at the door?" (needs gateway 1.1.21)

0.1.1 (2026-11-03)
- Cameras: each camera (and picture) card shows its picture, read again every 3 s while it is on the screen; click it to see it big
- Doorbell: when it rings (a doorbell event in Home Assistant), the page pops up the camera of the same device or room and the history notes it
- Only the buttons a device really supports are shown and accepted (Home Assistant's supported features) - e.g. no "Turn on" for a monitor that can't be switched on remotely
- Assistant access: "may control" no longer disappears for a high-risk device - it is greyed with the reason; every change says "saved"

0.1.0 (2026-11-03)
- First version: one page for your home through Home Assistant - rooms and devices with their state, live, and the controls that fit each kind; choose per device what the assistant may read or control (locks and water heaters at most read); a history of what was done; tools home_list, home_state, home_set (needs gateway 1.1.20: local service)

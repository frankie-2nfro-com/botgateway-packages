AI Meetings - change log
The top section is the coming version (no date): write each change below it as "- ...", in words
for the gateway owners - the Portal and the gateway show them as "What's new" before an update.
store_update.sh --package aimeetings publishes it (dated) and puts the next patch version on top; change
the top version by hand for a bigger step (e.g. 1.0.0). This file is not packed into the package.

0.1.3 (2026-11-24)
- "Ask one…" opens a box: choose who, write your question and choose how long the answer should be (about 30 to
  1000 words, for that answer only). Your question shows in the meeting as "@Name …".
- New in the right column: "Length of a turn" - move the slider to make every AI's next turns shorter or longer
  (30 to 1000 words) during the meeting.

0.1.2 (2026-11-19)
- On a Local gateway (2nfro Desktop 1.1.41: runs on this computer only) inviting people is not offered - the box
  says it needs a Connected gateway; and while no LLM is ticked in its Config, the meeting list says where to tick
  them. On a Connected gateway nothing changes.

0.1.1 (2026-11-17)
- The meeting starts with your opening statement, filled in from the purpose and the background: change it and
  press Send and start. When an AI asks another participant a direct question, that one answers next by itself.
  Each discussion round starts with the next participant.
- Your opening statement shows in the meeting as the chair's message, marked "Opening statement".
- New AI participants are named after your LLM workers' names in 2nfro Desktop (needs 1.1.38), and the LLM lists
  show "name (id)"; changing a participant's LLM changes its name too while you haven't typed your own.

0.1.0 (2026-11-17)
- First version: chair a meeting of AI models (each participant one of the LLMs you allow in the application's Config,
  with a name and a role) and invited people. Opening round (everyone at once, without seeing the others), discussion
  rounds (one after another), asking one participant, instructions that stay for the next turns, closing statements,
  stop and skip. A secretary model drafts the minutes and a running summary for long meetings; you edit and finish
  the minutes and export the meeting as Markdown. People join on the website with an invitation code and a name,
  read along, write and raise a hand; only you start the AIs' turns. Needs 2nfro Desktop 1.1.33 for several LLMs.

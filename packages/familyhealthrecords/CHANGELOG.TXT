FamilyHealthRecords - change log
The top section is the coming version (no date): write each change below it as "- ...", in words
for the gateway owners - the Portal and the gateway show them as "What's new" before an update.
store_update.sh --package familyhealthrecords publishes it (dated) and puts the next patch version on top; change
the top version by hand for a bigger step (e.g. 1.0.0). This file is not packed into the package.

0.2.4
- [Changed and non commited item put here]

0.2.3 (2026-11-04)
- Fixed: "Read again", "Delete" and "It is right" in an input's panel did nothing (the panel only opened again);
  a date changed there is now used too

0.2.2 (2026-11-04)
- The AI's health advice now always comes with each summary, in its own "AI advice" box (0.2.1 left it to the assistant,
  which often wrote none). For inputs read before, press "Read again" to get the advice

0.2.1 (2026-11-04)
- Choose the date of an input: after picking or dropping files you set the record's day (e.g. an old report added
  now gets the report's day); left empty, your assistant reads it from the file. Mood, sleep, text and height /
  weight keep the day you choose, and a date changed under "Read again" moves the input to that day
- Each area now opens on its overview, as in Aera: the latest day's inputs (with their time), the summary and the
  sub-categories with their number of records - tap one to see its records. The top bar has the menu (☰), the
  member's name (tap to change member) and ＋
- The summaries now include health advice from your assistant (AI). It is not checked by a doctor - ask your doctor
  before acting on it

0.2.0 (2026-11-04)
- New design after Aera: five areas at the bottom - body, diet, exercise, mind and care - each with its own
  sub-categories (blood test, blood pressure, food, sleep, prescription, follow-up ...). One input can be filed under
  several, e.g. a doctor's letter under doctor summary, prescription and follow-up
- Add more than files with ＋: mood (from → to), sleep quality, height / weight and text records with quick templates
  (blood pressure, blood sugar ...)
- Your assistant now also writes a short remark per area, compared with the earlier values ("比較兩星期前的血壓…"),
  and keeps a running summary per member. Inputs of one member are read one after another; the last 10 versions of the
  summary are kept and can be put back
- Chronic conditions per member (Members); a condition your assistant finds in a doctor's letter waits for you to confirm it
- Timeline (the days with inputs and that day's remarks) and My BMI (from the latest height and weight)
- Chinese / English on the page; a new tool health_summary for your assistant; what was kept in 0.1.0 stays

0.1.0 (2026-11-04)
- First version: your family's health documents per member, on your computer. Add photos or PDFs on the page or
  put them into the inbox folder (a sub-folder named after a member files them to that member); photos are made
  smaller without losing readable text. Your assistant reads each document (date, category, values with units and
  ranges); unclear ones wait for you to check. Records by year, values entered by hand, and read-only tools for
  the members you allow (needs gateway 1.1.22: storage and inbox)

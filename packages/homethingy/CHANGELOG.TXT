0.1.0 (2026-11-06)
- First version: your home's things - what you own, where it is (places within places: rooms, cupboards, boxes),
  its receipts, warranty cards and manuals - kept on your computer, for your own use only (no website)
- Add photos or PDFs (or drop them on the page): your assistant reads them - name, category, brand, model, serial
  number, price, shop, purchase date and warranty - and writes useful notes (how to claim the warranty, care).
  What you typed is never overwritten; other things on the same receipt can be added with one press
- Search as you type, filter by category, place, status, warranty and receipt; cards or a list; export to CSV
- Warranty page: what ends soon, what is still covered; lent out, put away, being repaired or gone
- Ask: "where is the drill?" - your assistant answers from your list
- Photos from the phone through the inbox folder (a sub-folder named after a place puts them there)
- Hide an item or a whole place from the assistant; the assistant's tools only read
